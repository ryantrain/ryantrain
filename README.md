## 🚀 About me

I'm a second-year Computer Science student at UofT who likes turning messy real-world problems into things people can actually use. I care about both sides of the build: the architecture underneath and the experience on top. Most of what I ship blends full-stack web/mobile with a bit of applied AI.

Right now I'm **open to internship and project opportunities** and always up for collaborating with people who care about doing quality work.
